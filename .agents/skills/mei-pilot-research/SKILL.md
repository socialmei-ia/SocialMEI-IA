---
name: mei-pilot-research
description: Plan and synthesize real-MEI pilot sessions using task-based usability evidence instead of generic satisfaction questions.
---

Before the session, record the business type, current tools/workarounds and the concrete tasks the participant performs today.

Use task prompts such as:
- cadastre produtos ou serviços reais do seu dia a dia;
- encontre um cliente;
- registre uma venda;
- mostre onde esperaria encontrar uma informação;
- realize uma tarefa que hoje faz no WhatsApp, papel ou planilha.

Observe completion, hesitation, terminology confusion, workarounds and missing capabilities.

Classify feedback as:
- bug;
- UX/usability issue;
- terminology confusion;
- missing feature;
- business-specific workflow;
- preference/idea.

Do not convert one participant's preference into a universal requirement.
Return evidence, severity, affected segment, product implication and recommended next validation.
