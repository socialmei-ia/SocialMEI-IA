---
name: visual-qa
description: Validate a SocialMEI visual change across responsive layouts, themes, interaction and accessibility without redesigning unrelated areas.
---

1. Identify changed screens/components and baseline behavior.
2. Test representative desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
3. Test Light and Dark; include Auto/Custom when relevant.
4. Check overflow, clipping, stacking, focus, keyboard, dialogs/drawers, touch targets and reduced motion.
5. Check console/page errors and failed local requests.
6. Compare before/after for unintended drift.
7. Report failures, skips and untested areas explicitly.
